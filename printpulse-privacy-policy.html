1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the PrintPulse - Print from anywhere mobile application (the "App", "we", "us", "our") handles information. PrintPulse lets you print PDFs, documents, photos, ID photo sheets, labels, planners and other printable templates from your Android device to Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and IP printers.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Files and photos you choose to print

When you pick a document, PDF or image (from your phone, gallery or cloud storage), the App reads that file only to preview, convert and send it to your printer. Files are processed on your device. We do not upload your files to our servers and we do not keep a copy.

2.2 Camera images

If you use the Document Camera Scanner, the App uses your camera to capture pages, apply filters and merge them into a PDF. Captured images stay on your device unless you choose to share or print them.

2.3 Printer and network information

To find and connect to printers, the App may access Wi-Fi and Bluetooth information, printer names and IP addresses you enter. This information is used only to establish the printing connection and to remember your printer for convenience. It stays on your device.

2.4 Content you type

Text you enter into templates, notes, invoices, certificates, cards, labels, QR codes or barcodes is used only to generate the printable output on your device.

2.5 Print history and settings

The App stores a local history of your prints and your preferences (theme, default printer, paper size, color mode) on your device so you can reprint and keep your settings. You can clear this data at any time from within the App or by clearing the App's storage in Android settings.

2.6 Web and email article printing

If you use the Web & E-mail Article Print tool, the App loads the web page or message you select so it can be formatted for printing. That page is requested directly from the website or service you choose, and that website's own privacy policy applies to it.

2.7 Information collected automatically

The App does not collect analytics, usage statistics or device identifiers.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Provide the printing, scanning, conversion and template features you request.
  • Connect to and communicate with your printer.
  • Save your history and preferences on your device.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your files, photos or scans for advertising or to train any machine learning models.

4. Permissions the App may request

  • Storage / file access (Scoped Storage and the Android Storage Access Framework): to open files you select and to save PDFs you create. The App only accesses the files and folders you explicitly grant.
  • Photos and media: to let you choose images from your gallery for printing and PDF conversion.
  • Camera: to scan documents. Used only when you open the scanner.
  • Bluetooth and nearby devices: to discover and connect to Bluetooth printers.
  • Wi-Fi, network state and Internet: to find network printers, print to an IP address, load web articles, and open cloud files.
  • Location (only if your version of Android requires it for Bluetooth or Wi-Fi discovery): used only to scan for nearby printers. We do not collect, store or share your location.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your device's Settings, although some features may then stop working.

5. Advertising

The App does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising identifiers.

6. Third-party services

The App works with services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Your printer manufacturer's software and the Android print system, when you print through them.
  • Cloud storage apps (for example Google Drive) when you open files from them.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

7. Data sharing

We do not receive or share your files, photos or scans. Data leaves your device only when you:

  • Send a print job to a printer (including through the Android print system);
  • Choose to share or save a file with another app or cloud service; or
  • Use the third-party services listed above.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

8. Data storage, retention and security

Files, history and settings are stored locally on your device. Temporary rasterization and spooler cache files created during printing can be removed using Settings > Clear Print Cache. Uninstalling the App deletes its locally stored data.

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Documents you print may be visible to anyone who has access to your printer's output or network, so please use trusted printers and networks.

9. Your rights and choices

You can at any time:

  • Delete print history and clear the print cache within the App.
  • Revoke permissions in your device settings.
  • Delete all App data by clearing storage or uninstalling the App.
  • Contact us to ask about, access, correct or delete any personal data we hold about you (see Section 13).

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights:

  • India: rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, including the right to access, correct and erase your personal data and to nominate a representative.
  • European Economic Area and UK: rights under the GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California, USA: rights under the CCPA/CPRA to know, delete and correct personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in some regions), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International use

Because files are processed on your device, they are not transferred across borders by us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
